Writing can live here as a full page, like this one, or it can link straight to a PDF.

**For a page:** write it below the `---` lines, just like a blog post.

**For a PDF:** put the file in `public/writing/`, then add a `file:` line at the top, such as `file: /writing/my-paper.pdf`. The card will open the PDF directly, and nothing needs to go underneath.
